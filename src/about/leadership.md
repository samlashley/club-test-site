The students behind Team Handball at UGA bring different backgrounds and levels of handball experience — each has found something meaningful through the club.

## Lourdes Calvo — Founder & President

<img class="bio-photo" src="/images/uploads/lourdes-calvo.jpg" alt="Lourdes Calvo">

- **Hometown:** Marietta, GA / Argentina
- **Major & Graduation Year:** Biomedical Physiology, Class of 2029
- **Preferred Handball Position:** Center Back / Wing

**Personal impact of handball:** Handball gave me a sense of belonging and helped me find a sport I genuinely enjoy. It also motivated me to get back on a healthier track.

**Advice for newcomers:** If you've never felt like you were "good at sports," this might just be the sport for you. Give it a chance!

## Victor Cristian — Vice President & Men's Team Captain

- **Hometown:** Marietta, GA
- **Major & Year:** Exercise & Sport Science B.S. / Nutritional Sciences M.S., Sophomore
- **Preferred Handball Position:** Center Back / Right Back

**Personal impact of handball:** Handball has been a great activity to keep me in shape while, more importantly, creating a group of some of the funniest and nicest people I have ever met.

**Advice for newcomers:** If you are concerned that you do not know anything, do not stress! A lot of people are probably in the same boat as you because handball is still growing in America, and we are here to teach you.

## Peyton Bergquist — Director of Recruitment & Outreach, Women's Team Captain

- **Hometown:** Suwanee, GA
- **Major & Graduation Year:** Regenerative Bioscience and Applied Biotechnology, Class of 2029
- **Preferred Handball Position:** Left Wing

**Personal impact of handball:** I love the people on the team and the connections I have made while playing the game. I played basketball, volleyball, soccer, and archery throughout my life, so finding handball at UGA was perfect.

**Advice for newcomers:** Don't be afraid to try something new! I didn't really know what handball was when I first started, but I'm so glad I joined. Get out of your comfort zone and have fun!

- **Preferred Handball Position:** Left Back / Right Back
