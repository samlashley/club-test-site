## Why Support Matters

As a growing club, many of our largest expenses come from tournament registration, travel, lodging, equipment, uniforms, and the costs of creating competitive opportunities for our players. Support helps us keep the sport accessible to students without placing the full financial burden on individual athletes.

## Our Supporters

**USA Team Handball** — Team Handball at UGA is proud to receive support from USA Team Handball as we work to grow the Olympic sport on UGA's campus and develop both our men's and women's programs.

## Ways to Help

- **Donate.** Support the club directly through our [GoFundMe]({{ site.gofundmeUrl }}).
- **Sponsor the club.** Businesses and organizations interested in supporting UGA Handball can [contact us](mailto:{{ site.contactEmail }}) about sponsorship opportunities.
- **Support tournament travel.** Contributions help offset registration, transportation, and lodging costs when our teams travel to represent UGA.
- **Support equipment & program growth.** Donations help the club purchase handballs, goals, uniforms, training equipment, and other resources as our men's and women's programs grow.
- **Partner with us.** We welcome opportunities to work with organizations interested in supporting collegiate athletics, Olympic sports, student involvement, or the growth of Team Handball in the United States.
- **Share our program.** Following and sharing UGA Handball on social media helps introduce the sport to more students and expands the community supporting our teams.

## Membership Dues

Membership dues are **$50 per semester**. Dues help support the general operation of the club, including equipment, practices, competition expenses, and other costs associated with maintaining both our men's and women's programs.

The club also actively fundraises throughout the year and has received generous support from individual donors — contributions that help reduce the amount individual players are responsible for paying.

## Tournament Costs

Players who choose to travel and compete may have additional expenses associated with tournaments. Before asking players to contribute, the club works to raise or secure funding to help cover:

- Tournament registration
- Lodging
- Transportation and gas
- Other necessary team travel expenses

Any tournament expenses that can't be covered through club funding, donations, sponsorships, or fundraising are divided among the participating players. The amount varies depending on the tournament, location, number of players traveling, and funding available.

Our goal is to keep expanding our fundraising and sponsorship efforts so that financial circumstances never prevent a student from having the opportunity to compete and represent UGA.
