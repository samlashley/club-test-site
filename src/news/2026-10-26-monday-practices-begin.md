Starting this week, we're adding a third practice to the schedule: **Mondays, 7:30–9:30 PM at the Ramsey Student Center, Gym West**, alongside our usual Fridays (5:30–7:30 PM) and Sundays (6:00–8:00 PM).

With tournament travel coming up this fall — a scrimmage against Atlanta Handball Club and Auburn, then the Tar Heel Invitational and Battle of the Bluegrass — the extra court time will help both our men's and women's teams keep building on last season's momentum.

No experience required, as always. Come find us at any of the three sessions.
