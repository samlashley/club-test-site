title: Hawks take the home opener 31–12
date: 2026-10-19
summary: Two tries from Eli Carter and a dominant scrum carried the Hawks past Riverside State.

The Hawks opened the home slate with a convincing 31–12 win over Riverside State on Saturday.

Fly-half Eli Carter scored twice, and the forwards won every scrum on our own feed. Thanks to everyone who came out to the Intramural Fields.

**Next up:** an away match at Pine Hill. Check the schedule for kickoff time.
