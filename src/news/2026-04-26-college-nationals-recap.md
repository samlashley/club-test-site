---
title: "Bronze at Nationals: UGA's First Trip to College Nationals"
date: 2026-04-26
summary: "Our combined men's and women's teams competed at USA Team Handball College Nationals for the first time, with the women's squad taking home a bronze medal."
---
From April 24–26, UGA Team Handball competed at USA Team Handball College Nationals for the first time — another major step for a program that only started competing in the 2025–2026 season.

On the men's side, a UGA/Auburn combined team (Michael Andrews, Victor Cristian, Huxley Ponchot, David Moseley, and Kyle McDole) finished **5th in Division 2**. Michael Andrews was also recognized as an **Academic All-American**.

On the women's side, Lourdes Calvo represented UGA as part of a combined team with Ohio State, earning a **bronze medal**. She was recognized as both an Academic All-American and a USATH All-Tournament selection.

It capped off a season that also included a bronze-medal finish at the Carolina Blue Cup in February — proof that a program built on partnerships with Auburn, Atlanta, DC, and Ohio State programs can already compete on the national stage.
